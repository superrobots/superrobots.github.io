Why a China focus?

China is a leading center of robotics research and development, with rapid progress in humanoids, embodied AI, and robots working at scale. We follow it as a window into some of today’s most advanced robotics, bringing Chinese sources into English so students can connect coding fundamentals with real-world innovation.
