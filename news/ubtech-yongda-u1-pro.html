Yongda Auto agrees to buy 400 UBTECH humanoids

The companies plan to use the U1 Pro for product demonstrations, sales promotion, and customer service, with delivery targeted by the end of 2026.

Announced 11 Nov 2026·Chinese news report·English brief

Chinese auto dealer Yongda Auto agreed to buy 400 U1 Pro humanoids under a partnership announced on November 11, according to The Paper. UBTECH is targeting delivery by year-end. The companies also plan to open a joint robot showroom in Shanghai.

Red Star News describes the full-body U1 Pro as a stationary robot designed for conversation. The proposed uses center on helping customers experience products and interacting with visitors at dealerships.

The coverage confirms the announced purchase and delivery target. It gives no confirmed count of units delivered to Yongda or final contract price.
