More than 300 robots join the everyday life of a theme park

AgiBot and Chimelong put robots to work across performances, visitor guidance, education, retail, and hospitality in Hengqin.

Reported 28 Sept 2026·Company announcement·English brief

AgiBot’s September 28 announcement describes a September 24 launch at Chimelong Spaceship park in Hengqin. The company says more than 300 robots are regularly deployed across seven application areas, including entertainment, education, visitor guidance, retail, and hotel services.

The announcement also marks the delivery of AgiBot’s 20,000th robot, an A3 Ultra, to Chimelong. The partners launched a joint tourism-and-robotics research institute and announced a 5G-A robotics application with China Mobile.

These are company-reported deployment figures. The announcement does not provide an independent assessment of reliability, operating cost, or how much human supervision each application requires.
