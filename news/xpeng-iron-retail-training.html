XPENG sets a 2027 retail training timetable for its IRON humanoid

XPENG says IRON will begin training as a store assistant in early 2027, with a public release targeted for the second quarter.

Announced 25 Nov 2026·Company announcement·English brief

XPENG presented its updated IRON humanoid at its November 25 AI Day in Guangzhou. The company also introduced Turing Physical AI Architecture, a framework it says connects perception, decisions and motion across its vehicles and robots.

XPENG plans to start training IRON in its own stores in the first quarter of 2027. It set a separate target for a public release in the second quarter. Both milestones are company targets.
