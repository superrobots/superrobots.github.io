Chengdu unveils a Gongga humanoid designed to replan household tasks

The fifth-generation Gongga is designed to remember where objects were, predict the results of a move, and revise its plan when its surroundings change.

Unveiled 11 Nov 2026·Chinese news report·English brief

The Chengdu Humanoid Robot Innovation Center unveiled its fifth-generation Gongga robot on November 11. Sichuan Online reports that the center designed its new control system for a household where people move things between tasks.

The center says Gongga combines color and depth cameras with information about its own position to update a 3D map. Its memory stores where objects were seen and what happened during earlier attempts. If an object is moved or a step fails, the robot can update its plan and use a model of the scene to predict the likely effects of its next move.

The reports describe the center's design and capability claims. They give no success rates across unfamiliar homes or timetable for consumer availability.
