X2 joins a hospital reception desk with Ant Afu

AgiBot reports a hospital deployment that answers visitor questions and helps people navigate outpatient services.

Announced 22 Oct 2026·Company announcement·English brief

AgiBot says an X2 humanoid integrated with Ant Afu is working at Peking University People's Hospital. It draws on hospital information to answer routine questions about departments, visits, and directions.

The announcement cites hospital operating figures through September 30: 46,686 interactions over 66 working days, averaging 707 a day. These are reported usage counts, rather than an independent evaluation of answer accuracy.

The deployment illustrates a public-service role for an embodied interface: helping visitors find information and their way around a busy building.
