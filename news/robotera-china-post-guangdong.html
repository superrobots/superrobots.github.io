ROBOTERA reports humanoid deployments at 11 Guangdong postal hubs

The robot maker says its China Post deployment now spans eleven hubs in eight cities, following a pilot in Guangzhou.

Reported 17 Nov 2026·Company announcement·English brief

ROBOTERA announced on November 17 that humanoid robots were deployed at eleven hubs run by China Post's Guangdong branch. The company traced the rollout to a March pilot in Guangzhou.

The work includes parcel induction, which feeds items into a sorting line. ROBOTERA disclosed the number of sites, while the number of robots at those sites remains undisclosed.
