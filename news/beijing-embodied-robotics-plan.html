Beijing plans real-world tests for embodied AI robots

The city's 2026–2028 plan calls for a shared test site and aims to collect more than one million hours of robotics data across at least 100 real-world settings.

Issued 11 Nov 2026·Official policy document·English brief

Beijing's municipal industry bureau issued its 2026–2028 action plan for embodied AI robots on November 11. The plan calls for a test site with several kinds of robots. Organizations that use robots would propose tasks, and teams building AI models would try to complete them under a common evaluation process.

Beijing aims to collect more than one million hours of data across at least 100 real-world settings. The plan also calls for pilots in manufacturing, warehousing, retail, and public services, plus a testing and certification system covering safety and reliability.

The policy took effect on November 11. The test site, data collection and application pilots are program goals through 2028.
