AgiBot’s A3 gets a native NetEase Cloud Music integration

Voice-controlled music is scheduled to reach the full-size humanoid on October 15. Music-generated movement remains a development goal.

Announced 30 Sept 2026·Company announcement·English brief

AgiBot says its A3 humanoid now integrates NetEase Cloud Music at the system level, connecting voice requests, music search, account authentication, and playback. The company plans to make voice song requests available from October 15, with account capabilities dependent on delivered software and authorization.

AgiBot reports pilot testing at Chimelong Spaceship park. Generating gestures from music, an AI DJ, and interactive singing are described as future development directions. Public or commercial music playback requires separate licensing; the pilot does not provide blanket commercial authorization.
