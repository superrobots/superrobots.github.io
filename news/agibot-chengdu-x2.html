AgiBot X2 robots take visitor-service roles in Chengdu

Three X2 humanoids serve visitors at Kuanzhai Alley, with roles spanning welcome desks, cultural interpretation, and retail.

Announced 22 Oct 2026·Company announcement·English brief

AgiBot says it has partnered with Chengdu Culture & Tourism Group to deploy three X2 humanoids at Kuanzhai Alley. Their jobs include answering visitor questions, explaining local culture, and presenting and handing over retail products.

The company says the retail system uses a vision-language-action model, which connects what a robot sees and understands with its movements. It reports support for more than 20 product types, over 98% task success, and roughly 30 seconds per task.

Those performance figures come from AgiBot. The announcement does not establish how the system performs across other shops or how much supervision it needs.
