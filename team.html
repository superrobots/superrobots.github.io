About SuperRobots

We are SuperRobots — a competitive FIRST LEGO League (FLL) Challenge team (Team #70620) dedicated to engineering intelligent robots, conducting applied research, and advancing STEM education through hands-on innovation.

Our team combines expertise in mechanical design, autonomous programming, data-driven research, and strategic problem-solving to compete at regional and state-level FLL tournaments. We build technology that tackles real-world challenges — and we share what we learn through open-source projects and educational content.

Competition Seasons

Explore our competitive history and open-source work on GitHub.

2023 — SuperPowered

FIRST LEGO League SuperPowered challenged teams to explore how energy is generated, stored, distributed, and consumed. Teams designed, built, and programmed LEGO robots to complete missions on a themed field, focusing on innovative solutions for energy challenges in our world.

  • Learned about renewable and non-renewable energy sources and sustainable engineering.
  • Designed and built a robot integrating motors and sensors to complete complex, energy-themed autonomous missions.
  • Developed a research project to improve energy efficiency in our community using data analysis.
  • Practiced teamwork, iterative problem-solving, and professional presentation skills.

Learn more about SuperPowered

2024 — Masterpiece

FIRST LEGO League Masterpiece invites teams to imagine and innovate in the world of creative arts and entertainment. Teams use STEM and robotics to solve challenges related to sharing their passions and talents with the world, making performances and art more accessible and engaging.

  • Explored how software technology and AI can enhance creative expression and communication.
  • Built and programmed robots using advanced coding to complete autonomous missions inspired by the arts.
  • Created a community project to help people share their stories and talents digitally.
  • Strengthened collaboration, creativity, and technical programming skills.

Learn more about Masterpiece

2025 — Unearthed (Current)

FIRST LEGO League Unearthed is the 2025-2026 season challenge, where teams will dig into the fascinating world beneath our feet! Teams will explore how we interact with the ground, discover underground mysteries, and use STEM and robotics to solve real-world problems related to geology, archaeology, and the environment.

  • Investigate underground resources and how environmental science impacts our daily lives.
  • Design and program robots with precise navigation algorithms to complete missions inspired by digging, discovery, and preservation.
  • Develop innovative, tech-driven solutions for sustainable use of underground spaces.
  • Enhance teamwork, systematic research, and creative computational problem-solving skills.

Learn more about Unearthed
